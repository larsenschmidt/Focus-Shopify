# The repertoire · by language and phase

One master at a time, two weeks each, read whole. Set the current one in `config.json`. The speaking repertoire is kept apart from the writing one.

## English
- I: Bacon, the Essays. Lincoln, Gettysburg and the Second Inaugural. Orwell, "Politics and the English Language", "Shooting an Elephant". E. B. White, "Once More to the Lake".
- II: Hazlitt, "On Going a Journey". Johnson, the Rambler essays. Douglass, "What to the Slave Is the Fourth of July?". Didion, "On Keeping a Notebook". Baldwin, "Notes of a Native Son". Lewis Thomas, "The Lives of a Cell".
- III: Montaigne (Frame or Screech). Swift, "A Modest Proposal". Woolf, "The Death of the Moth". Annie Dillard, "The Writing Life". Paul Graham's essays. Churchill and King, for the room.

## Spanish
- I: Martí, "Nuestra América" and the crónicas. Azorín, "Castilla". Machado, the poems and "Juan de Mairena".
- II: Ortega y Gasset, "Meditaciones del Quijote". Borges, "Otras inquisiciones" and the prologues. Alfonso Reyes, "Visión de Anáhuac". Unamuno, the essays.
- III: Paz, "El laberinto de la soledad". García Márquez, the Nobel lecture and "Vivir para contarla". Javier Marías, the columns. Ibargüengoitia. For the room: Borges, "Siete noches".

## Italian
- I: Calvino, "Marcovaldo", then "Lezioni americane". Ginzburg, "Le piccole virtù".
- II: Primo Levi, "Il sistema periodico". Eco, "La bustina di Minerva". Leopardi, "Operette morali", in small doses.
- III: Pasolini, "Scritti corsari". Manzoni, for the long sentence that never loses you.

## Danish
- I: Andersen, the tales. Weekendavisen, for the living essay.
- II: Tove Ditlevsen, "Barndom". Herman Bang, "Ved Vejen".
- III: Kierkegaard, "Enten–Eller", excerpts. Inger Christensen, "Alfabet", for the ear.

## Chinese
- I: graded readers at 1,000 then 2,000 words; nothing native yet. 朱自清, "匆匆", when the first 2,000 hold.
- II: 朱自清, "背影". 老舍, "济南的冬天". 汪曾祺, the food essays.
- III: 鲁迅, "朝花夕拾". 余华, "我们生活在巨大的差距里".

## French
- Maintenance at one hour a week: Saint-Exupéry, "Terre des hommes". Camus, "Noces", "L'Été".
- When French gets a daily hour: Montaigne in the original, Pascal, Voltaire's letters.
