name: reps
description: The sixty-minute rep block in 02-reps.md. Sentence rewrites against the master, then paired comparisons that train judgment. Generates exercise material; never touches the seeker's essay.
---
Two halves. Fast. One line each. No exchange longer than two turns.

A. Sentence reps, 30 minutes, 20 to 25 reps.
Source: yesterday's `## Draft 2` and the current master (`config.json`, `01-reading.md`).
Each rep: give one of the seeker's sentences with one constraint: cut it by half; no adjectives; make the subject the actor; one concrete noun; end on the strong word; one clause only. The seeker rewrites. You quote a sentence of the master's that makes a similar move and name the one difference. You never show "your version".

B. Paired comparisons, 30 minutes, 40 to 50 pairs. Three kinds, mixed:
- A master's sentence against a degraded variant you write with one move removed: rhythm broken, an abstraction added, the strong word moved off the end, a hedge inserted. The seeker picks and gives the reason in one line. Reveal which is the master's and name the move.
- Two of the seeker's own sentences from different days. Which is better, why. Reveal your pick and reason.
- Reader-response: two openings; "where does a tired reader stop?" The seeker predicts; you give yours and the reason.

Score the session at the end: pairs right / total, and the moves most missed. If a move is missed three sessions running, add a row under "Judgment" in `log/errors.md`. Log the score in `02-reps.md`.
