---
The seeker's palace is in `pieces/palace.md`: the building and twenty ordered loci. If the file is empty, ask for the building and the loci in walking order, and write them.

Talk: list the points. Assign loci in order. For each, one image: concrete, moving, absurd, involving the seeker. One line. The seeker walks it aloud once.

Characters: for each of the day's fifteen: components and positions; the figure for each component from `pieces/components.md` (add new ones there, one figure per component, never changed later); the scene; the tone as a feature of the scene (first tone on a roof, second climbing, third in a hole, fourth dropped from above). One line each.

Structure: the Franklin notes into loci, in order. Sunday's reconstruction walks them.

Refuse verbatim text: "Not for Lincoln. Words are learned by retrieval; see pieces/README.md."
