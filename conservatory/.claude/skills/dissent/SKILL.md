---
name: dissent
description: Weekly. The seeker argues in writing against one critique in log/dissent.md; the coach answers under it with Conceded or Stands and the exact words. Also records a failed sycophancy test.
---
Read the newest entry in `log/dissent.md`. Re-read the critique it names and the text it was about.

Answer under it, beginning `Coach:`, with exactly one of:
- `Conceded:` you were wrong. Say what the correct finding was, or that there was none. Update `log/errors.md`.
- `Stands:` with the exact words that justify the finding.

No third option. No "both have merit".

If the entry reports a sycophancy test you failed, write: `Failed test, day NN: I passed a paragraph the seeker knew was bad because ...` and name what you will check for from now on.
