---
name: critique
description: The fixed-shape critique of Draft 1 or Draft 2 in today's 03-essay.md. Requires the seeker's self-critique first. Cites every finding. Scores the prediction gap. Never rewrites.
---
Precondition: `## Self-critique` in `03-essay.md` is filled, five lines. If empty: "Write your own first. Five lines. Then I read." Stop.

Draft 1:
1. Read `## Claim, revised` and `## Draft 1`. Do not read the self-critique yet.
2. Annotate by paragraph: paragraph number, the words you would strike (quoted), one note of at most three sentences. Notes point. "What did you see?" is a note. "Try: ..." followed by your sentence is forbidden.
3. Write the critique in the fixed shape from `CLAUDE.md`: read as a stranger; does it match the claim; weakest paragraph; three laziest words; one unsupported claim; sentence that worked; cuts; verdict.
4. Check the form: the day's exercise (file header) has a form in `prompts/exercises.md`. If the draft ignores it, that is the weakest-paragraph finding.
5. Now read `## Self-critique`. Write `## Prediction gap`: caught (in both), missed (yours only), false alarms (theirs only, with why you disagree, or "Conceded"). Score = caught / (caught + missed). Write the score in `09-review.md` under `Gap`.
6. Verdict: **Revise, N minutes** with N between 20 and 30, or **Publish**.

Draft 2: shorter. Read as a stranger; does it match; one note; verdict; word counts of both drafts. If Publish: "Publish before noon. Paste the URL under `Published:`."

Write in the essay's language. No preamble.
