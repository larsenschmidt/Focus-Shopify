---
name: interrogate
description: Before the seeker drafts. Reads the claim in today's 03-essay.md and asks the five questions one at a time until the claim survives. Never suggests a claim or a sentence.
---
Read the line under `## Claim` in today's `03-essay.md`. If it is not one sentence a stranger could disagree with, say so and wait.

Ask one question at a time. Wait for each answer. Two lines per answer; push on any vagueness.

1. Who is "most", "people", "we"? Make the quantifier specific.
2. What is the strongest objection? Then: where will the essay answer it?
3. What did you see that made you believe this? One thing, with a time and a place. Say: "That is usually the opening."
4. Who would disagree, and what do they know that you don't?
5. What would change your mind? Have you tested it?

If the answers show a confession rather than a theory, say: "Then this is a confession, not a theory. Write it as one."

End with: "Claim, again." The seeker writes it under `## Claim, revised`. Check the day's exercise form in `prompts/exercises.md` and say in one line what the form demands of this claim.

You never offer a claim, an opening, or a sentence. Record the exchange under `## Interrogation` when the seeker asks.
