# The road · phases with gates

You move when the gate opens, not when the calendar says. The coach checks these at the Sunday review with evidence from the logs.

## Phase 0 · Baseline · sealed
`days/day-00/`. Nothing corrected, everything kept. Re-run every thirty days in `days/day-NN/baseline/`.

## Phase I · Foundation · weeks 1–12
**Aim.** The loop runs daily without breaking. Tics become visible and named. The fear leaves. Twelve pieces held, eighty-four essays, a hundred and sixty-eight recordings.
**Exercises.** Essays cycle the first six forms (fable, narrative, chreia, maxim, refutation, confirmation). Franklin reconstruction weekly. Impromptu three minutes. Prepared talk seven.

Gate to Phase II:
- [ ] Sixty consecutive days of the core. A bad day counts if the core was done.
- [ ] The three named writing patterns absent from critique five days running.
- [ ] Impromptu: claim inside fifteen seconds and fewer than ten fillers per three minutes, five days running.
- [ ] Twelve pieces in the old tier, clean, said not recited.
- [ ] Italian: a paragraph re-expressed with two corrections or fewer, three times running.
- [ ] Prediction gap: seven-day mean above 0.7 on the essay critique.

## Phase II · Imitation · weeks 13–36
**Aim.** Range, by stealing deliberately.
**What changes.** Essays 600–900 words. Forms seven to twelve. A weekly pastiche: the same essay in the manner of two masters, then your own. Impromptu five minutes, prepared talk ten. Pieces 300–500 words. Italian promoted to deep if its gate opened. Portuguese may enter the rotation.

Gate to Phase III:
- [ ] A human reader, given three of your essays and three by others, blind, picks yours.
- [ ] A ten-minute prepared talk with no notes whose transcript publishes without editing.
- [ ] One essay of 2,000 words you would still publish thirty days later.
- [ ] Chinese: a 300-character paragraph with three corrections or fewer.
- [ ] Prediction gap: seven-day mean above 0.85.

## Phase III · Voice · weeks 37–78
**Aim.** Something only you would say, said as only you would say it.
**What changes.** Forms thirteen and fourteen. A series: ten essays on one question. Video scripts cut from the essays. A real room monthly. You teach one person. You write the rubric; the coach enforces yours.

Gate to Phase IV:
- [ ] The ten-essay series, published, with a reader who argued back.
- [ ] A talk to a room of strangers, recorded, whose transcript publishes untouched.
- [ ] Italian writing at the Phase I English standard.

## Phase IV · Performance · week 79 on
No gate out. Weekly essay, a video every two weeks, a talk a month, a long thing in progress. The coach's job is selection, not correction. The baseline still runs every thirty days.

## Every seventh week · light
No new essays. Reread the corpus. Revise the best three. Recite everything you hold. Read. `newday.py` marks it.
