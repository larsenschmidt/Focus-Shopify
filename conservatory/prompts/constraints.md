# Constraint drills · one a week · Saturday, after the long essay

Take one paragraph of the week's best essay and write it again under one constraint. Queneau did it ninety-nine ways. Control comes from the cage.

- No adjectives.
- Monosyllables only.
- Without the letter a (or e).
- As a telegram, under forty words.
- As a sermon.
- As a legal clause.
- As a child would tell it.
- In one sentence.
- In sentences of exactly seven words.
- Starting every sentence with a verb.
- Without the verb to be.
- As a question.
- In the second person.
- Backwards: last event first.
- In the weak language first, then back into the strong one (the differences are the critique).
